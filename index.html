About the Artist

Mehak Mittal is a self-taught watercolor artist whose artistic journey spans over ten years and across three continents.

Inspired by movement, nature, and vibrant color, she creates expressive artwork blending watercolor with ink.

Her work continues evolving through commissions, exhibitions, immersive experiences, and joyful celebrations.

“Painting for me is a form of meditation.”
