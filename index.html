About The Artist

Mehak Mittal is a self-taught watercolor artist whose journey spans over ten years and across three continents. Inspired by movement, color, and nature, she creates expressive artwork blending watercolor and ink.

Her work has been exhibited internationally and continues evolving through commissions, celebrations, and immersive artistic experiences.

“Painting for me is a form of meditation.”
